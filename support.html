Support

Antenna IPTV is a free, private player for M3U playlists and XMLTV guides on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Getting started

  1. Open Antenna IPTV and accept the notice.
  2. Tap Add a playlist. Enter a name and the playlist URL (or choose a file).
  3. Optionally add an XMLTV guide URL. If the playlist announces one itself, Antenna IPTV uses it.
  4. Pick a channel to start watching.

Frequently asked questions

Does Antenna IPTV include channels?

No. Antenna IPTV ships with no channels and never suggests any. You add playlists you are entitled to use.

“A server with the specified hostname could not be found” or “Could not connect to the server”

The address in your playlist does not answer. Open it in Safari: if Safari cannot open it either, the provider is down or has changed address, and you need a new URL from them.

“No channels were found”

The address did not return an M3U playlist. A single HLS stream (.m3u8) is accepted and played as one channel.

A channel does not play

Open the engine menu in the player and try the other engine: AVPlayer (HLS, Picture in Picture, AirPlay) or VLC (.ts, RTSP, UDP, DASH and more). Antenna IPTV can switch to VLC automatically when AVPlayer fails (Settings).

The guide is empty or off by some hours

Use Settings → Guide → Time shift. Use “Reload guide” after changing the guide URL.

Keyboard shortcuts (Mac, or iPad with a keyboard)

↑/↓ change channel · digits jump to a number · Space play/pause · M mute · F full screen · L channel list · I programme info · ⌫ last channel · Esc close.

Is my data safe?

Your playlists and settings stay on your device, and private-playlist passwords are kept in the Keychain. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and what the app connects to.

Can I add a playlist that needs a username and password?

Yes. When adding or editing a playlist by URL, fill in Private playlist. Prefer https:// addresses: with http:// your login travels unencrypted, and the app warns you.

“The file is larger than the … MB limit”

To protect memory, Antenna IPTV rejects very large playlists and guides. Raise Settings → Safety limits if your file is legitimately bigger.
