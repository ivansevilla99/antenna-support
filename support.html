Support

Antenna is a free, private player for M3U playlists and XMLTV guides on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Getting started

  1. Open Antenna and accept the notice.
  2. Tap Add a playlist. Enter a name and the playlist URL (or choose a file).
  3. Optionally add an XMLTV guide URL. If the playlist announces one itself, Antenna uses it.
  4. Pick a channel to start watching.

Frequently asked questions

Does Antenna include channels?

No. Antenna ships with no channels and never suggests any. You add playlists you are entitled to use.

“A server with the specified hostname could not be found” or “Could not connect to the server”

The address in your playlist does not answer. Open it in Safari: if Safari cannot open it either, the provider is down or has changed address, and you need a new URL from them.

“No channels were found”

The address did not return an M3U playlist. A single HLS stream (.m3u8) is accepted and played as one channel.

A channel does not play

Open the engine menu in the player and try the other engine: AVPlayer (HLS, Picture in Picture, AirPlay) or VLC (.ts, RTSP, UDP, DASH and more). Antenna can switch to VLC automatically when AVPlayer fails (Settings).

The guide is empty or off by some hours

Use Settings → Guide → Time shift. Use “Reload guide” after changing the guide URL.

Keyboard shortcuts (Mac, or iPad with a keyboard)

↑/↓ change channel · digits jump to a number · Space play/pause · M mute · F full screen · L channel list · I programme info · ⌫ last channel · Esc close.

Is my data safe?

Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
