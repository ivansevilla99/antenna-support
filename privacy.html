Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Short version: Antenna collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers of ours.

What Antenna stores

Your playlists, guide settings, favorites, recent channels and app settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What Antenna connects to

Antenna only contacts the addresses you add: your playlist URLs, your guide (XMLTV) URLs, the stream addresses inside your playlists, and the channel logos those playlists reference. Those servers are run by third parties and can see your IP address and what you request, like any other app or browser would. Antenna does not add identifiers, and it does not send them anything about you beyond what a normal request needs (and the User-Agent you choose in the settings).

Tips

Antenna is free and has no ads. If you leave an optional tip, the payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or any personal data. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to that purchase.

Data we collect

None. Antenna does not collect, share or sell personal data, and it includes no third-party analytics or advertising software.

Content

Antenna is a player. It includes no channels and never suggests any. You are responsible for loading only content you are entitled to watch.

Children

Antenna does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the support page on GitHub.
